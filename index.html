ABOUT LIVINGSPRING

Warm timber. Cool tile. A shaded verandah for morning coffee.

LivingSpring is a peaceful compound tucked into a quiet corner of Sunyani. Each apartment is fully self-contained — a proper kitchen, generous bedrooms, and a private outdoor space to sit with a book or a plate of food.

Whether you're in town for work, a family visit, or a slow week away, LivingSpring is a home away from home.
